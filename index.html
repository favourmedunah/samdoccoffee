Our Story

From a dream on Alli Street to a movement across Nigeria.

Where It Began

A Small Street.
A Big Dream.

It started on 15 Alli Street, Emaudo, Ekpoma — a quiet corner in a city full of energy. In 2025, Samdoc Coffee & More opened its doors with a simple but powerful belief: that Nigerians deserve a café experience that is world-class, warm, and truly their own.

We noticed a gap. Students and young professionals in Ekpoma wanted a space that felt premium — somewhere they could work, connect, laugh, and refresh. A place where the coffee was real, the food was satisfying, and the atmosphere said: "You belong here."

So we built it. From the carefully sourced coffee beans to the handcrafted recipes on our menu, every detail at Samdoc was designed to say something bigger — that excellence doesn't have to come from Lagos or Abuja. It can start right here in Ekpoma.

This is just the beginning. Samdoc Coffee & More is growing — and we're taking every city in Nigeria with us, one cup at a time.
